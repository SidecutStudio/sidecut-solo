# Review a phone layout

**Request** “Does the settings page feel cramped on a phone?”

**Setup** An isolated preview with sample data, at 390 and 768 pixels in the default theme.

**Walkthrough** Open the notifications section at both widths. Compare adjacent controls, the space between sections and the placement of the section heading. Check the narrow layout again after a reload.

**Illustrative finding** At 390 pixels, the section heading and preceding help text sit closer together than the labels inside that section. The narrow layout makes the heading look like a continuation of the previous section. Increase the space above the heading using the product's section spacing token. The form remained readable and the controls could be reached, so this observation is about clarity.
